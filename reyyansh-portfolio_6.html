Orchestrating Multimodal Pipelines for Automated AI Video Generation ↗

A systems-level study of an LLM-orchestrated, six-stage video generation pipeline evaluated across 30 short-form briefs. Staged orchestration raised mean quality scores from 2.7 to 4.1 versus single-pass generation, and a CLIP-based validation loop cut prompt-misalignment failures from 47% to 20%.

Rating, Color, and Opening Choice: An Empirical Analysis of 15,467 Rated Chess Games ↗

An empirical study of rated online chess games testing how opening choice, Elo predictions, and White's first-move advantage behave at amateur level — finding 42-point win-rate spreads across openings at club level and a first-move edge that vanishes above 2000.
